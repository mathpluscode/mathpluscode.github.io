## Student @ Centre for Medical Image Computing (CMIC), UCL

### Automatic Segmentation of Liver Laparoscopic Videos

> Master Thesis, Apr. 2019 - Sep. 2019.

The main interest is to perform automatic segmentation for laparoscopic videos during liver resection surgery. This ongoing research project is mainly based on [previous work](http://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/1563392/1/Gibson_Deep_residual_networks.pdf) on the laparoscopic videos. The objective is to improve the performance (achieving higher dice score) and efficiency (using fewer data) of the previous method.
